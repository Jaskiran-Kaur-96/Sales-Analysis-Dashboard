# 📊 Superstore Sales & Profit Analysis Dashboard


### 1. Project Title / Headline

📈 Superstore Sales & Profit Analysis Dashboard

A comprehensive and interactive Power BI dashboard built to analyze sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, and regional trends using the Superstore Sales Dataset. The dashboard enables stakeholders to monitor key business metrics and uncover insights that drive strategic decision-making.

---

### 2. Short Description / Purpose

The Superstore Sales & Profit Analysis Dashboard is a visually engaging Power BI report designed to provide a complete overview of sales and profit performance across products, customers, categories, and regions. The dashboard helps business analysts, managers, and decision-makers identify growth opportunities, track business performance, and understand profitability trends.

---

### 3. Tech Stack

The dashboard was built using the following tools and technologies:

• 📊 Power BI Desktop – Main data visualization platform used for report creation.
• 🐍 Jupyter Notebook – Used for data cleaning, preprocessing, exploratory data analysis (EDA), and insight generation.
• 📂 Power Query – Data transformation and preparation within Power BI.
• 🧠 DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – Used for KPI calculations, profit margins, and dynamic measures.
• 📝 Data Modeling – Relationships established across sales, customer, product, and regional data.
• 📁 File Format – ".pbix" for development and ".png" for dashboard previews.

---

### 4. Data Source

Source: Superstore Sales Dataset

The dataset contains transactional sales records including orders, customers, products, categories, sub-categories, regions, discounts, sales, and profits. It is widely used for business intelligence and analytics projects to explore sales performance, profitability, and customer purchasing patterns.

---

### 5. Features / Highlights

• Business Problem

Retail businesses generate large volumes of sales data every day, making it difficult to identify performance trends and profitability drivers without an analytical solution.

Key questions such as:

- Which product categories generate the highest sales?
- Which regions contribute the most profit?
- Who are the top customers?
- How do discounts impact profitability?
- Which products are causing losses?

...are difficult to answer using raw transactional data alone.

• Goal of the Dashboard

To deliver an interactive business intelligence solution that:

- Tracks sales and profit performance.
- Monitors customer and product trends.
- Identifies loss-making products.
- Analyzes the impact of discounts on profit.
- Supports data-driven business decisions.

• Walkthrough of Key Visuals

Executive Summary Dashboard

Provides a high-level overview of business performance through key KPIs:

- Total Sales: $2.3M
- Total Profit: $286K
- Total Orders: 5K
- Profit Margin: 12.5%

Sales by Product Category

Compares sales performance across Technology, Furniture, and Office Supplies categories.

Monthly Sales Trend

Displays month-wise sales performance and seasonal trends throughout the year.

Top 10 Customers

Highlights the customers contributing the highest sales revenue.

Top 10 Products

Ranks the best-performing products based on sales value.

Sales by Region

Map visualization showing geographical sales distribution across the United States.

Profit Analysis Dashboard

- Loss Making Products Table
- Discount vs Profit Analysis
- Profit by Region
- Profit by Category
- Profit by Sub-Category

These visuals help identify profitable segments, loss-making products, and the impact of discounts on business performance.

• Business Impact & Insights

Sales Performance Monitoring: Track overall business growth using sales, profit, and order KPIs.

Customer Analysis: Identify high-value customers and strengthen customer retention strategies.

Profit Optimization: Discover loss-making products and reduce profitability leakage.

Regional Analysis: Compare sales and profit performance across regions.

Pricing Strategy Evaluation: Understand how discounts affect profitability.

Data-Driven Decision Making: Enable stakeholders to make informed strategic decisions using actionable insights.
